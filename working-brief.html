How I work / A practical resource

A clear project starts with a clear brief.

A public, simplified project-framing worksheet. Use it to align the outcome, the decision, the people, and the evidence before expanding the plan.

Download the one-page PDF ↓Back to my approach

01 / Purpose & outcome

What problem are we solving, for whom, and why now? Define one primary outcome and how it will be measured.

02 / The decision

What decision or resource is needed? Name the decision-maker, deadline, and consequence of delay.

03 / Scope & boundaries

What is included? What is explicitly excluded? Capture the assumptions that would change the plan.

04 / Milestones & ownership

List the major milestones, accountable owners, and the evidence required to move forward.

05 / Risks & dependencies

For each material risk or dependency, name an owner, needed-by date, mitigation, and escalation point.

06 / Success measures

Define the baseline, target, measurement method, and review cadence. Separate activity from outcomes.

07 / Operating rhythm

Agree how progress, decisions, and changes will be recorded. Keep one current source of truth.

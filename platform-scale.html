The challenge

More testing capacity is only valuable if teams can depend on the results. Scaling the lab required attention to infrastructure capacity, service levels, ownership, and the quality of the data reaching stakeholders.

My contribution

  • Defined operating metrics, service levels, and capacity plans for distributed validation environments.
  • Coordinated engineering, infrastructure, product, and operations stakeholders around platform priorities.
  • Scaled automated lab infrastructure and benchmarking capabilities.
  • Established managed-service practices including SLAs, runbooks, and clear responsibilities.

The outcome

The TPM résumé records lab scaling from 18 to 43 robots while maintaining 96%+ platform uptime. These figures describe the robot-lab expansion; they are not interchangeable with the separate device-fleet growth figures.

Working principle

Capacity and operational readiness belong in the same plan. A larger platform must still be dependable.

Executive summary

Luis Borges Moreno is a senior technical program manager with 21+ years directing NPI, infrastructure, and AI hardware programs across Meta, Verizon, and Nokia. His career spans cellular testbeds, GPU and rack-scale data center builds, RAN and 5G, and the analytics layers that turn telemetry into a decision leadership can act on. Teams and sponsors engage him when a program has momentum but lacks the structure to convert it into shipped, reliable capacity.

He has guided organizations from a single prototype lab to a 36-person, 8-workstream function, owning priorities, Go/No-Go gates, vendor delivery, and the data that tells the truth about a schedule. At Meta he ran the 47-project, 9-partner-organization NPI portfolio and the QoE lab that became the internal standard other hardware teams still use — landing 94% of P0s while deprioritizing the rest in writing, with a reason and a sponsor. He holds an MBA and a BSEE.

About Luis Borges

Technical depth. Product perspective. Program ownership.

From telecom systems and product portfolios to research infrastructure and dependable platforms.

The through-line

I’m Luis Borges, a Senior Technical Program Manager with a background in Product Management and systems engineering. My career has moved from deploying telecom networks to shaping product portfolios and building the programs that research and product teams depend on.

At Nokia, I worked across packet-core engineering, technical delivery, product support, and product management. At Verizon, I focused on 5G systems engineering. At Meta, I brought that foundation to research infrastructure, benchmarking platforms, and AI-enabled workflows.

That progression shaped how I work: understand the system, identify the customer need, and make the decisions and responsibilities clear enough for a team to deliver.

What I own

I own the program: the roadmap, scope, dependencies, stakeholder alignment, operating cadence, and readiness decisions. Engineering partners own the technical design and implementation.

For Codec Avatars, that meant recovering a network-delivery plan. For Project Aria, it meant making a security-sensitive request clear enough to approve. For the QoE platform, it meant growing both capacity and the operating model behind it.

Leadership in practice

At Meta, I grew a two-person lab into a 36+ person function across eight workstreams. The work was not just adding capacity. It was establishing ownership, service expectations, and a review cadence that could keep pace with demand.

I favor a visible plan over a reassuring status report. Teams should be able to see dependencies, trade-offs, and changes early—not discover them at the deadline.

Governance should grow with the team. A useful process makes a decision or a handoff easier; it should not exist merely to add another meeting.

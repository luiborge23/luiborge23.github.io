Research infrastructure · Meta

Re-plan the path. Recover the date.

How I aligned research and infrastructure teams around a network dependency for Codec Avatars—and moved delivery from nine months to four.

Senior Technical Program ManagerProgram ownership · Cross-functional delivery

The challenge

Codec Avatars research depended on a network path into the Reality Labs research cluster. The research and infrastructure organizations ranked the dependency differently, and the original delivery plan no longer had the staffing it needed.

The issue was not simply a late circuit. A delayed connection meant accelerator capacity could sit idle while research waited.

What I owned

I owned the plan of record, staffing coordination, vendor engagement, executive communication, and delivery date. Engineering owned the technical network design. This was a capacity and connectivity path into the data center—not a data-center fabric design program.

The decision

I reframed the escalation around the consequence of delay: idle research hardware. Rather than asking one organization to accept the other’s priority label, I rebuilt an executable path that both could support.

I re-engaged vendors, coordinated engineering backfill, and replanned delivery. I maintained a weekly plan of record with named owners and translated network milestones into a clear executive view of when research capacity would become usable. The connectivity decision weighed dark fiber, a wavelength lease, a carrier circuit, and reallocation of existing capacity. The trade-off was additional cost for greater schedule control.

Make the impact clear

Connect schedule delay to idle research capacity.

Rebuild the plan

Align staffing, vendors, and connectivity options.

Earn agreement

Give both organizations a delivery plan they can support.

The outcome

Nine months → four months

The delivery timeline moved from nine months to four. Reality Labs moved from executive escalation to endorsing the plan, and the priority conflict was resolved.

What I took forward

When a dependency loses its people, re-plan the path before re-planning the date. Alignment is stronger when the solution removes the conflict rather than asking one team to lose.
